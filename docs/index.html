A common rule of thumb

Investor-education material often pairs how soon you need money with how much is in stocks. The shorter the time, the less a bad year can be waited out. It is a general starting point, not a recommendation for you.

Money needed inMix often shownWhy
About 1 yearCash-likeLittle time to recover from a drop.
About 5 yearsConservative to moderately conservativeSome growth, but a bad year still hurts.
About 10 yearsBalancedLong enough to ride out most downturns.
About 15 yearsGrowthStocks have usually led over this span.
20+ yearsGrowth to all stocksThe most time to recover from crashes like 2008.
